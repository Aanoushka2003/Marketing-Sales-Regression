Marketing Spend and Sales — Linear Regression Analysis

Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between marketing promotional spending and sales revenue across TV, radio, and social media campaigns.

The primary objective is to identify the marketing channel with the strongest linear relationship with sales and quantify that relationship using ordinary least squares (OLS) regression.

Business Question

Which marketing channel is the strongest predictor of sales, and what does the relationship imply for promotional budget decisions?

Dataset

The dataset contains four variables:

TV — TV promotional budget

Radio — Radio promotional budget

Social_Media — Social media promotional budget

Sales — Sales generated by the promotion

Dataset source: Saragih, H. S. (2020), Dummy Marketing and Sales Data.

Methodology

The analysis follows these stages:

Data loading and inspection

Missing-value assessment and treatment

Exploratory data analysis

Relationship visualization using pairplots and scatterplots

Predictor selection

Simple linear regression using OLS

Regression-assumption diagnostics

Coefficient and model-fit interpretation

Business recommendations

Key Findings

TV promotional spending shows the strongest positive linear relationship with Sales in the dataset.

The fitted simple linear regression model is approximately:

Sales = -0.1263 + 3.5614 × TV

The model has an R-squared of approximately 0.999, meaning that TV spending alone explains approximately 99.9% of the observed variation in Sales within this dataset.

The estimated TV coefficient is approximately 3.5614, with a 95% confidence interval of approximately [3.558, 3.565].

Business Implication

TV is the strongest candidate for further investigation when considering promotional budget allocation. However, the regression demonstrates association rather than causation. A high R-squared should not be interpreted as proof that increasing TV expenditure will necessarily cause a proportional increase in sales.

A useful next step would be to build and compare a multiple linear regression model using TV, Radio, and Social Media simultaneously.
